Count set

The set a child counts: toys on a rug, one per cell, each stamped with its number when touched.

Overview

Touching a toy counts it: Pibbo says the next number and the toy keeps a numbered badge. Nothing is ever cleared; on a retry Pibbo counts them again aloud, lighting each in turn.

Anatomy

  1. Rug4:3, Count tint, 32px radius
  2. ToyA picture in one cell of a 4 × 3 grid
  3. BadgeThe number said, 44px disc

Variants

One variant; its states are what changes.

Sizes

The rug grows to fit the space left under the prompt, never past it, so nothing scrolls.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; the product gets them from :hover, :focus-visible, :active and the real attributes.

Theming

Rug is --color-count-soft; badge --color-count-strong ringed in --color-art-light. Pictures sit on a white plate in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A labelled group of buttons; a counted toy becomes disabled and its label says its number.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNext toy not yet counted

Focus

Shared ring on the toy.

Motion

Highlight moves toy to toy at 500ms per number; reduced motion shows the badges only.

Touch

Toys at least 60px; cells never overlap.

Content

  • The cast is a set of things to count, not rules.
  • Up to 12 things per rug.
