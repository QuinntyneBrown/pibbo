Lesson card

Today's lesson and the check card: big coloured cards that start a guided run.

On Home the check card sits beside the group pictures, as tall as they are, and wraps under them on the narrowest phones (.home__row, .home__check .check-card): the “?” glyph above the title “What do I know?”, spoken when touched, with no second line, since everything shown is also said (L2-024 #4, journey 23).
