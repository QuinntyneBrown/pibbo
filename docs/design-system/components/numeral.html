Numeral

Overview

Numerals are toggle buttons in a group of choices (usually three). Choosing one presses it; Check then checks it. The child can change their mind until Check. Numerals use Andika so 1 and 7 are drawn the way a child is taught.

Anatomy

  1. NumeralAndika 48px bold
  2. Pressed tileCount colour, white numeral, edge sinks

Variants

The answer outline marks the right numeral after the third wrong try, together with Pibbo saying it.

DesktopDigit keys are not shortcuts; Tab and Enter only, so a stray key never answers.

Theming

White tile on any background; pressed reads --color-count-strong. The answer ring reads --color-success.

Accessibility
