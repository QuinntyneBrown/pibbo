Group switch

The two big group pictures on Home, Count and Read: touching one speaks its name and shows its activities.

Overview

The group switch is a pair of toggle buttons. One is always pressed; it shows a check mark as well as the subject colour, so the choice never depends on colour alone. The older segmented .switch__option (quiet tabs) survives only on grown-up surfaces.

Anatomy

  1. GlyphAndika 24px bold: 1 2 3 or a b c
  2. NameFredoka 20px semibold, spoken on touch
  3. Pressed markA check mark plus the subject colour

Sizes

Group options are at least 96 × 96px; they grow with their label.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; the product gets them from :hover, :focus-visible, :active and the real attributes.

Theming

Unpressed options use the surface; pressed ones use --color-count-strong or --color-read-strong with the white label.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Two toggle buttons with aria-pressed inside <nav aria-label="Groups">. Pressing one releases the other.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves between the two
Enter / SpacePresses the focused option

Focus

Shared ring; focus stays on the option after it is pressed.

Labelling

The visible name; the glyph is aria-hidden.

Motion

Touch

96px or more; 16px apart (--gap-targets).

Content

  • The group's name only: Count, Read.
  • Spoken on touch from a recording.
