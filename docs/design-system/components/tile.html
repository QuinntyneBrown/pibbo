Tile

Letter and word tiles: the pieces a child hears, builds words with, or picks.

Overview

Tiles carry letters (Sound it out, Spell by sound) and words (Build a sentence). Text is Andika, lowercase first, with a single-storey a and g. A tile that has been used stays in place, dimmed, so nothing jumps.

Sizes

One size per kind; a wide tile holds a letter group (sh, igh, oa): two or three letters that make one sound, one outline, one recording, at least 60 × 60 px (L2-014).

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; the product gets them from :hover, :focus-visible, :active and the real attributes.
