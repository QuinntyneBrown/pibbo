Answer bar

The bottom bar of every round: Check, then what happened (right, or let's try again).

Overview

The answer bar holds Check while the child chooses. After Check it says what happened, in words and with Pibbo's voice, and offers the next step. Retry is calm: lilac, never red, and nothing is cleared.
