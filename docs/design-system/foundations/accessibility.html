Overview

Every component page states role, keyboard, focus, labelling, contrast, motion and touch.

Child-first commitments

  • Targets at least 60 × 60px with 16px between.
  • One thing at a time; nothing scrolls in an activity.
  • Whatever is said is shown; whatever is shown is said.
  • Colour is never the only carrier of a state.
  • Lowercase first; single-storey a and g.
  • Calm: no flashing, motion stops.
