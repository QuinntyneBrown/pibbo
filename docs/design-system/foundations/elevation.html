Foundation

Elevation

Pibbo is flat with one signature: the press edge, a solid 5px ledge under anything a child taps.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark
  • Overview
  • Press edge
  • Shadows

Overview

The press edge (--press-depth) shows what can be touched; it sinks 4px when pressed. Soft shadows are rare and reserved for grown-up overlays.

Press edge

Rest, pressed, and the white variant whose edge reads --color-depth.
