Typography

Two families: Fredoka is Pibbo's voice; Andika is for anything a child reads to learn, with single-storey a and g.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark

Overview

Fredoka (400–700) carries buttons, prompts and titles. Andika (400, 700) carries numerals, letters and words a child reads. Both are bundled in assets/fonts/.

Usage

  • Lowercase first in anything a child reads.
  • Never below 14px; child-facing text never below 18px.
  • Prompts step from 20px to 24px at 40rem; type never scales with the viewport.
  • Numerals in Andika so 1, 4 and 7 are plain.

Accessibility

Sizes in rem so they follow the user's settings. Line heights 1.15–1.6 survive WCAG 1.4.12 text spacing.
