Color

Every colour comes from one palette (docs/design-system/palette-source.md): warm neutrals, purple, blue, green, pink, red and navy, plus white and black.

Overview

Colour does a job in Pibbo: each subject owns one (Count is blue, Read is pink), green means a right answer, lilac is the calm colour of rewards and of trying again. Colour is never the only carrier: a pressed choice also shows a check, a right answer also says so.

The palette was changed on 2026-11-27 from the prototype's sun, berry, sky and leaf to the ramps in palette-source.md. Only those colours (and white and black) are used. The prototype had no ramp shade for yellow, so Pibbo's body and the stars moved to lime and lilac; see the README changelog.

Secondary text

Dark is built from the darker shades of the same ramps: the page is purple-950, surfaces neutral-900 (lighter, so they read as raised), wells black. Text and accents lighten (purple-200, blue-200, pink-200, green-200).

Usage

  • Subject colour goes on the one action and the progress fill, nowhere else.
  • Retry is lilac, never red. Red and pink errors are for grown-up surfaces only.
  • Text on a tint uses the matching -fg role, never the strong button colour.
  • Pictures need a plate in dark (--color-picture-plate).
