# Journey 3 · Count a set: transcript

Voice `af_heart`. The source is [`narration.json`](narration.json).

1. **hook** — Count the toys: your child touches each thing, and Pibbo counts along.
2. **touch** — One touch, one number. Each toy says the next number and shows it, marked by shape as well as colour. Touch it again, and the count doesn't move.
3. **widths** — On a phone or a wide desktop it's the same round, with more room and no more things.
4. **ask** — When every toy is counted, Pibbo asks how many, and offers numerals to choose from.
5. **retry** — A wrong answer is a chance to try again. Nothing is taken away: Pibbo counts the toys again, and your child chooses again.
6. **yes** — The right one earns a short yes, and the next round is ready straight away.
7. **proof** — The tests prove it: one touch, one number, a kind retry, and nothing to scroll.
8. **close** — Pibbo helps young children learn to count and to sound out words. A child opens it on whatever screen is to hand, picks an activity, and plays: counting the things in front of them, and saying each sound of a word and blending the sounds into the word.
