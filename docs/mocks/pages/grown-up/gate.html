Skip to the gate

For grown-ups

Touch seven, three, nine in order.

A wrong number goes back to Pibbo.

The numbers are written as words and chosen at random each time; nothing is spoken, so a child who cannot read cannot pass by chance (fewer than one try in 500). A wrong number or Back to Pibbo returns to Home as it was.
