Offline (L2-033, ADR 0032). An activity opened once while online is kept on the device and opens and plays as usual. Touching one that was never opened speaks its name as usual, then greys its picture and draws the no-network symbol (a crossed-out signal) on it; it does not open and nothing else changes. Grey is never the only sign: the symbol is drawn and the picture is named “needs the internet” to a screen reader. When the device is online again the picture is shown as usual. Nothing is shown before a touch: Home looks the same online and offline.
