Touching a group picture speaks its name (a recording) and shows its activities, at most 6 per screen: when a group has more than 6, a screen shows 5 and More, which speaks “More” and shows the next screen (the last one’s More goes back to the first). Touching an activity picture once speaks its name and opens it. Only activities that exist are shown. The Lesson picture beside the wordmark is the chosen group’s guided lesson: touching it speaks “Lesson” and opens the lesson path where the child left off. The check game has its own picture, “What do I know?”, which is spoken when touched and opens it. The padlock alone in the top corner is the grown-up entry, at least 48 px from every activity picture; touching it speaks nothing. Every target is at least 60 × 60 px with 16 px between targets.
