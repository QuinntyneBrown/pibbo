The landing page, as the prototype draws it (ADR 0035). The top bar shows the child’s name and stars; both are sample data (“Maya”, 26 stars), and the padlock beside them is the grown-up entry, which speaks nothing. Today’s lesson card names the chosen subject’s guided lesson, shows how many of its games are done, and its button (“Keep going”, or “Start” before the first game) is spoken and opens the lesson where the child left off. The check card, “What do I know?”, is spoken and opens the check game. Reading and Counting switch the subject: each is spoken when touched. The activities are listed in sections, each card with its glyph, its name and up to three stars; touching one speaks its name and opens it. Home scrolls; every target is at least 60 × 60 px with 16 px between targets, in 2 columns on a phone, 3 from 640 px and 5 from 1024 px, and the page stops growing at 1280 px.
