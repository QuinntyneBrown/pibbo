About Pibbo

Pibbo helps young children learn to count and to sound out words. A child cannot yet read, so everything is spoken as well as shown.

Pibbo has no accounts, adverts, purchases, chat, notifications or tracking, and asks for no microphone, camera or location. Everything it keeps stays on this device.
