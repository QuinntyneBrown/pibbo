# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Pibbo is a responsive web application (Angular frontend, .NET backend) in which a child who
cannot yet read practises counting and sounding out words. Each L1 below realises one or more of
the user journeys listed in its heading note. Vocabulary follows `AGENTS.md`: child, grown-up,
activity, round, thing, number, numeral, word, letter, sound, blending, spoken, recording.

## L1-001: Count a set
Journey: *Count a set*. Pibbo shall let a child count a set of things by touching each one once,
hearing the next number spoken for each, and then choosing the numeral for how many there are.

## L1-002: Count and write to twenty
Journey: *Count and write to twenty*. Pibbo shall let a child count sets of up to twenty things
and form the numeral for the count.

## L1-003: Compare two numbers
Journey: *Compare two numbers*. Pibbo shall let a child choose which of two numbers is bigger,
supported by a picture of each quantity.

## L1-004: Count backward
Journey: *Count backward*. Pibbo shall let a child count down from a starting number.

## L1-005: Skip-count
Journey: *Skip-count*. Pibbo shall let a child count by twos, fives and tens.

## L1-006: Add two numbers together
Journey: *Add two numbers*. Pibbo shall let a child, taking turns with another person on the same
device, combine two sets and find the total.

## L1-007: Practise counting through music
Journey: *Practice counting through music*. Pibbo shall let a child chant numbers in order to a
musical beat, with on-screen movement to copy.

## L1-008: Match letters to sounds
Journey: *Match letters to sounds*. Pibbo shall let a child connect letters with the sounds they
make, where each letter makes its sound and never its name.

## L1-009: Recognise letter forms
Journey: *Recognize letter forms*. Pibbo shall let a child match uppercase and lowercase forms of
the same letter.

## L1-010: Sound out and blend a short word
Journey: *Sound out a short word*. Pibbo shall let a child hear each sound of a three-sound word
and blend the sounds into the word.

## L1-011: Spell a word by sound
Journey: *Spell a word by sound*. Pibbo shall let a child choose the letter for each sound of a
spoken word.

## L1-012: Read letter groups that make one sound
Journeys: *Read two letters as one sound*, *Read three letters as one sound*, *Read vowel
teams*. Pibbo shall let a child read a group of two or three letters (digraphs, trigraphs and
vowel teams) as one sound within a word.

## L1-013: Read adjacent consonants
Journey: *Read adjacent consonants*. Pibbo shall let a child hear and say both sounds of
adjacent consonants at the start or end of a word.

## L1-014: Read silent-e words
Journey: *Read silent-e words*. Pibbo shall let a child hear how adding a final e changes a word
and its vowel sound.

## L1-015: Read longer words by syllable
Journey: *Read longer words*. Pibbo shall let a child read a two-syllable word one syllable at a
time and then as a whole.

## L1-016: Connect words to pictures
Journey: *Connect words to pictures*. Pibbo shall let a child read a set of related words and
match each to its picture.

## L1-017: Recognise common words
Journey: *Recognize common words*. Pibbo shall let a child practise high-frequency words and
meet them again in later rounds.

## L1-018: Build a short sentence
Journey: *Build a short sentence*. Pibbo shall let a child put words in order to make a short
sentence that matches a picture.

## L1-019: Hear rhymes
Journey: *Hear rhymes*. Pibbo shall let a child find words that rhyme with a spoken word.

## L1-020: Follow a guided lesson
Journey: *Follow a guided lesson*. Pibbo shall offer guided lessons that lead a child through a
fixed sequence of counting or reading activities.

## L1-021: Check my progress
Journey: *Check my progress*. Pibbo shall offer a check game that shows a child what they can do,
without scores that compare, shame or take anything away.

## L1-022: Choose what to practise
Journey: *Choose what to practise*. Pibbo shall let a child choose a counting or reading activity
themselves from a Home screen that needs no reading.

## L1-023: Shared round behaviour
Every activity shall follow the same round rules: spoken and shown instructions, one thing asked
at a time, wrong answers lead to a retry, brief praise, no timers, lives or losing.

## L1-024: Content and recordings service
The .NET backend shall serve the activity content (words, letters, sounds, pictures, recordings,
lessons) to the frontend, and shall hold nothing about any child.

## L1-025: Privacy and child safety
Pibbo shall collect nothing about a child, shall have no accounts for children, adverts,
purchases, external links, chat, notifications or tracking, and any progress shall stay on the
device.

## L1-026: Security
Pibbo shall be protected against the OWASP Top 10, with validated input, hardened transport and
headers, and authenticated access to any content-administration functions.

## L1-027: Performance and resilience
Pibbo shall load quickly on modest phones and networks, respond to every touch immediately, and
keep working when the network drops after content is cached.

## L1-028: Responsive layout
Every screen shall work on phones, tablets, desktops and large screens (XS through XL), in either
orientation, without scrolling during an activity.

## L1-029: Accessibility
Pibbo shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA, be usable by a child who cannot read or cannot hear, and work with
mouse, keyboard and touch.

## L1-030: Grown-up area
Settings and information meant for a grown-up shall sit behind a gate a child will not pass by
accident, and never between the child and an activity.

## L1-031: Observability and operations
The backend shall expose health checks, structured logs and metrics that contain no data about
any child, and be deployable repeatably.
