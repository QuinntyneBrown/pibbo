# L2 - Detailed Requirements

Sample words and numbers in these criteria ("map", "sun", "ship", 7, 14...) are the cast used by
acceptance tests, not the full content set. "Touch" means a tap, a click, or Enter/Space on the
focused item. Every acceptance test names the L2s it covers in a header comment
(`// Traces to: L2-012`).

## L2-001: One touch, one number
**Traces to:** L1-001

A Count round shows N things (1-10). Touching an uncounted thing speaks and shows the next number and marks the thing counted; touching a counted thing does not change the count.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a round with 5 toys and none counted, when the child touches toy 1, then "one" is spoken, the numeral 1 is shown on that toy, and the toy is marked counted by shape and colour, not colour alone.
2. Given toys 1-3 counted, when the child touches a counted toy again, then the count stays at 3 and no number is spoken.
3. Given two fingers touch two different uncounted toys at the same instant, when the touches are processed, then each toy is counted once and the count rises by exactly 2.
4. Given all 5 toys counted, when the count completes, then 3 to 5 numeral choices including 5 are offered, each at least 60 x 60 px.

---

## L2-002: Answer how many
**Traces to:** L1-001

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given all toys counted and numerals offered, when the child chooses the correct numeral and touches Check, then its number is spoken (when it is chosen), brief praise plays, and the next round is offered.
2. Given the same state, when the child chooses a wrong numeral and touches Check, then nothing is taken away, Pibbo re-counts the toys aloud highlighting each in turn, and the child chooses again.
3. Given a round is generated, when it is laid out, then no two things overlap and each is a separate target of at least 60 x 60 px.

---

## L2-003: Count to twenty
**Traces to:** L1-002

Rounds of 11-20 things are grouped in ten-frames so they fit one screen without scrolling.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a round of 14 things on a 360 x 640 px viewport, when it is shown, then all 14 are visible without scrolling, each at least 60 x 60 px, as one full ten-frame and one partial ten-frame.
2. Given 14 things counted, when the count reaches 14, then "fourteen" is spoken and the child is asked to write the numeral.

---

## L2-004: Write the numeral
**Traces to:** L1-002

The child writes the numeral by touching digit tiles 0-9 in order, then Check; no handwriting or drag is needed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the count is 14, when the child touches 1 then 4 and then Check, then "fourteen" is spoken and the answer is accepted.
2. Given the count is 14, when the child touches 4 then 1 and then Check, then the answer is not accepted, the tiles clear, and Pibbo shows 1 then 4 being touched as a hint, saying each digit; then the child writes again.
3. Given the count is 7, when the child touches 7 and then Check, then the answer is accepted without a second digit.
4. Given two digits entered, when a third digit is touched, then it is ignored.
5. Given a digit tile is touched, when it is written, then the digit is spoken and shown in the next box, and Clear empties the boxes.

---

## L2-005: Compare two numbers
**Traces to:** L1-003

Two different numbers from 0 to 20, each shown as a numeral and as a set of dots in ten-frames. The child touches the card they think is bigger, then Check.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given 6 and 9 are shown, when the child touches 9 and then Check, then "nine is bigger than six" is spoken and shown, and praise plays.
2. Given 6 and 9 are shown, when the child touches 6 and then Check, then the sets stay lined up one-to-one in their ten-frames, the 3 extra dots in the set of 9 are highlighted (by shape as well as colour), "Look at the dots. Which has more?" is spoken and shown, and after Try again the child chooses again.
3. Given 100 generated rounds, when sampled, then no round has equal numbers and the bigger number is on the left in 40-60% of rounds.
4. Given a card is touched, when it is touched, then its number is spoken and the card is shown as chosen; touching the other card chooses it instead.

---

## L2-006: Count backward
**Traces to:** L1-004

A start number from 5 to 20 is shown; the child chooses each next number down from three numerals until reaching 0. A track shows the numbers said so far.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given start 10, when the round begins, then "ten" is spoken and shown as the start.
2. Given start 10, when the child chooses 9, 8 ... 0 in order, then each number is spoken as chosen and shown on the track, and praise plays at 0.
3. Given the current number is 7, when the child chooses 5, then nothing advances, "Going down from 7… try again." is spoken and shown, and after Try again the child continues from 7.

---

## L2-007: Skip-count by twos, fives and tens
**Traces to:** L1-005

The child hops along a number line from 0 in steps of 2, 5 or 10, choosing where each hop lands from three numerals; a switch changes the step and starts the line again from 0.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given step 2, when the child chooses 2, 4, 6 ... 20 in order, then each number is spoken as chosen and shown on the number line.
2. Given step 5, when counting, then the sequence runs 5, 10 ... 50.
3. Given step 10, when counting, then the sequence runs 10, 20 ... 100.
4. Given step 5 and the sequence 5, 10, when the child chooses 14, then nothing advances, "Count on 5 more from 10." is spoken and shown, and after Try again the child chooses again.

---

## L2-008: Add two numbers together (pass and play)
**Traces to:** L1-006

Two players share one device. Each player in turn adds a set of 1-5 things by touching + (and − to take one away), and touches Done to pass the turn; Pibbo then asks for the total (2-10), the players choose a numeral and touch Check. No names, avatars or scores are recorded.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a new round, when it starts, then each player's turn is shown and spoken by a symbol and colour (a star, a moon), never by a name, and only the player whose turn it is can add.
2. Given player 1 added 3 and player 2 added 4, when both sets are shown, then "how many altogether?" is spoken and 7 is among 3-5 numeral choices.
3. Given the total is 7, when 6 is chosen and checked, then all things are counted aloud from 1 to 7, "Count all the ducks together, one by one." is shown, and after Try again the players choose again.
4. Given the round ends, when it completes, then no winner, loser or score is shown.

---

## L2-009: Count to music
**Traces to:** L1-007

A rhythm plays while a figure makes a simple move on each beat; the numbers 1-10 are spoken and shown on the beat for the child to chant along to. The child starts the music, can pause it and keep dancing, and chooses Slow or Fast. No microphone or camera is used.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the round has started, when each beat plays, then the next number is spoken, its numeral is shown and marked in the row of numbers, and the figure moves once.
2. Given the device sound is off, when the round plays, then the numeral and a visual beat pulse still show each count.
3. Given reduced motion is on, when the round plays, then the figure does not move and the numeral change alone shows the beat.
4. Given the round is playing, when the child touches Pause, then the music and numbers stop within 200 ms, and Keep dancing resumes from the same number, saying the next one.
5. Given the round's audio, when measured, then its integrated loudness does not exceed -16 LUFS and matches the other recordings within 2 LU.
6. Given any music round, when it runs, then no microphone or camera permission is requested.
7. Given the round is playing, when the tab is hidden, then the music and motion stop, and nothing plays again until the child touches Keep dancing.
8. Given the last number is said, when the dance ends, then "You danced all the way to 10!" is spoken and shown, and Next goes back to Home.

---

## L2-010: Match letters to sounds
**Traces to:** L1-008

**Acceptance Criteria:**
Four letters are shown in one column and their four sounds, each with a picture of a word it begins, in another, in a different order. The child touches a letter, then the sound it makes.

1. Given the letters m, s, t, c and their sounds, when the child touches m and then the sound /m/, then /m/ is spoken, and m and /m/ are joined in the same colour, marked as matched and no longer take a touch; when all four are matched, then brief praise is spoken and shown and Next is offered.
2. Given any letter, when touched, then it is held and its sound ("mmm") is spoken, never its name ("em"); given any sound, when touched, then its recording is spoken.
3. Given t is held, when the child touches the sound /k/, then /k/ is spoken, the sound gives a small shake (none with reduced motion), t stays held, and nothing is taken away; there is no timer and no limit on tries.
4. Given every consonant recording in the content set, when audited against the content checklist, then each is the sound alone with no added vowel.

---

## L2-011: Recognise uppercase and lowercase
**Traces to:** L1-009

**Acceptance Criteria:**
Four uppercase letters are shown in one column, headed Big, and their four lowercase forms in another, headed Small, in a different order. The child touches a big letter, then its small letter.

1. Given the big letters A, B, D, G and the small letters a, b, d, g, when the child touches B and then b, then b's sound is spoken, and B and b are joined in the same colour, marked as matched and no longer take a touch; when all four are matched, then brief praise is spoken and shown and Next is offered.
2. Given any letter, big or small, when touched, then its sound ("b") is spoken, never its name ("bee"); a touched big letter is held.
3. Given D is held, when the child touches b, then b's sound is spoken, b gives a small shake (none with reduced motion), D stays held, and nothing is taken away; there is no timer and no limit on tries.
4. Given any round, when rendered, then letters use the house letterforms (Andika): single-storey a and g, and b/d/p/q and I/l/1 clearly distinct.

---

## L2-012: Sound out and blend a three-sound word
**Traces to:** L1-010

A word of three single-letter sounds ("map") is shown as letter tiles. The child touches each letter in order to hear its sound, then blends, and then chooses the word's picture from three.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given "map" is shown, when the child touches m, a, p in turn, then "mmm", "a", "p" are spoken and each letter is highlighted as it sounds and shows its sound beneath it.
2. Given all three sounds have been heard, when the child touches blend, then the sounds are spoken again back to back, each letter lit as it sounds, "map" is spoken, and three pictures are offered.
3. Given not every letter has been heard, when the round is shown, then blend is offered but not yet active, and the next letter to touch is marked.
4. Given a recording is playing, when another letter is touched, then the playing recording stops and the new one starts within 100 ms.
5. Given three pictures are offered, when the child chooses the picture of "map" and touches Check, then that picture is highlighted, brief praise plays, and the next round is offered; a wrong picture follows `L2-026`.

---

## L2-013: Spell a word by sound
**Traces to:** L1-011

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given "sun" is spoken and shown with its picture, three empty boxes and up to 6 letters, when the child touches a letter, then its sound is spoken and it fills the next empty box, which is marked.
2. Given a filled box, when the child touches it, then its letter is taken out and that box is the next to fill.
3. Given a box's speaker or an empty box, when touched, then the sound that belongs in that box is spoken.
4. Given every box is filled, then the letters take no touch, "Does it say the word? Check it!" is spoken and shown, and Check is ready.
5. Given s, u, n in the boxes, when the child checks, then each sound and then "You spelled" and "sun" are spoken, the boxes show right, and "s – u – n. You spelled “sun”!" is shown with Next.
6. Given s, u, m in the boxes, when the child checks, then "Say it slowly. Which sound doesn’t match?" is spoken and shown with Try again; Try again takes out only the letters that do not match, and nothing else is lost.

---

## L2-014: Letter groups that make one sound
**Traces to:** L1-012

Digraphs (sh, ch, th, ck), trigraphs (igh, tch) and vowel teams (oa, ai, ee) are shown as one joined tile that makes one sound.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given "ship" shown as [sh][i][p], when the child touches [sh], then one recording "shhh" plays, not "s" then "h".
2. Given "night" shown as [n][igh][t], when sounded out and blended, then three sounds play and "night" is spoken.
3. Given "boat" shown as [b][oa][t], when blended, then "boat" is spoken.
4. Given a letter-group tile, when displayed, then its letters share one tile outline and the tile is at least 60 x 60 px.

---

## L2-015: Adjacent consonants
**Traces to:** L1-013

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given "stop" shown as [s][t][o][p], when the child touches s then t, then "sss" and "t" are each spoken separately.
2. Given the question "what are the first two sounds?" with choices "st", "s" and "t", when "st" is chosen, then both sounds are spoken and praise plays; when another is chosen, then both sounds are modelled and the child chooses again.

---

## L2-016: Silent-e words
**Traces to:** L1-014

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given "cap" with its picture, when the child touches the e tile, then the word becomes "cape", the a tile shows a change mark, "cape" is spoken, and the picture changes to a cape.
2. Given "cape", when the child touches the e tile again, then e is removed and "cap" is spoken.
3. Given a round asking for the picture of "cape", when the cap picture is chosen, then "cap" is spoken and the child chooses again.

---

## L2-017: Read longer words by syllable
**Traces to:** L1-015

A two-syllable word ("sunset") is shown as one tile for each syllable, under the rule "sun · set Read one beat at a time". A syllable is shown and spoken to the child as a "clap". The child reads each syllable in order, then blends, and then chooses the word's picture from three.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given "sunset" shown as [sun][set] with `Tap “sun” and clap it.`, when the child touches [sun], then "sun" is sounded out and blended ("sss", "u", "nnn", "sun") with its tile lit, "clap 1" is shown beneath it, and [set] is then asked for and read likewise.
2. Given both syllables read, when the child touches blend, then "sun", "set" and "sunset" are spoken and three pictures are offered; when the child chooses the picture of "sunset" and touches Check, then that picture is highlighted; a wrong picture follows `L2-026`.
3. Given a 320 px wide viewport, when a two-syllable word is shown, then both syllable tiles fit on one line without scrolling or truncation.

---

## L2-018: Connect words to pictures
**Traces to:** L1-016

A round shows related words on one theme (such as "by the sea") in one column and their pictures in another, in a different order: two to four pairs, as the mock draws. The child touches a word, then its picture.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given words and pictures shown, when the child touches a word and then its picture, then the word is spoken, and the two are joined in the same colour, marked as matched and no longer take a touch; when the child touches a word and then another picture, then the word and the picture's word are both spoken, the picture gives a small shake (none with reduced motion), the word is let go, and they stay apart with nothing taken away.
2. Given any word in the round, when it is touched, then it is held and its sounds are spoken in turn as in L2-012 ("b", "oa", "t"), never the letters' names, for the child to blend; given no word is held, when a picture is touched, then its word is spoken.
3. Given all pairs joined, when the round ends, then brief praise naming the theme is spoken and shown ("You read all the by the sea words!") and Next is offered.
4. Given any pairing, when made, then it needs two touches and no drag; there is no timer and no limit on tries.

---

## L2-019: Recognise common words
**Traces to:** L1-017

Common words ("the", "is", "a") are found by sight in a short story (Spot the word, ADR 0024). A common word missed in a round comes back in a later round while the activity is open.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a round asks for "the", when it is shown, then "Find every “the” in the story." is spoken and shown, "the" is shown large, one empty dot stands for each "the" in the story, and every word of the story is a touch target of at least 60 × 60 px with nothing scrolling.
2. Given the round, when the child touches a "the" (or "The"), then "the" is spoken, that word stays highlighted and takes no further touch, and one more dot is filled.
3. Given the round, when the child touches another word, then it greys for 0.4 s with no sound, and nothing found is lost.
4. Given every "the" is found, then "You found “the” 5 times!" is spoken and shown with Next, briefly, and the story takes no further touch; after 5 rounds Next returns to Home with Read chosen.
5. Given a common word missed in a round, when the next 5 rounds are chosen, then that word is asked in at least one of them.
6. Given what was missed, when the child leaves the activity, then it is forgotten: nothing is stored on the device or sent anywhere. Whether Pibbo remembers practice is not decided (`AGENTS.md`).

---

## L2-020: Build a short sentence
**Traces to:** L1-018

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a picture and 3-5 shuffled word tiles ("the", "cat", "sat"), when the child touches them in the order "the cat sat", then each word moves to the next slot and the whole sentence is spoken.
2. Given the next slot expects "cat", when the child touches "sat", then "sat" is spoken, stays where it is, and the next slot is pulsed (outlined under reduced motion).
3. Given any word tile, when the child touches it before placing, then the word is spoken.
4. Given the sentence complete, when shown, then it starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop.

---

## L2-021: Hear rhymes
**Traces to:** L1-019

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a round starts, when it is shown, then "cat" is shown with its picture and its ending marked, with four cards ("hat", "sun", "bat", "map") in an order the random source chooses; "Which words rhyme with “cat”? Find them all." is spoken and shown, and Check takes no touch.
2. Given a card, when shown, then it has a picture and its word; when touched, then "cat" and then that card's word are spoken, and the card is chosen and ticked, or unchosen if it was chosen, so the round needs no reading and more than one card can be chosen.
3. Given at least one chosen card, then Check takes a touch; given none, it takes none.
4. Given exactly "hat" and "bat" are chosen, when Check is touched, then both show as right, "Cat, hat, bat — they all end in “at”!" is spoken and shown as praise, and Next is offered; after the fifth round Next returns to Home with Read chosen.
5. Given any other choice, when Check is touched, then "Listen to the ends of the words: c-at…" is spoken and shown with Try again, the cards take no touch, and Try again lets the child choose afresh.
6. Given the third wrong Check in one round, when it is made, then "Listen: cat, hat, bat — they all end in “at”." is spoken and shown, "hat" and "bat" show as right, and Next completes the round (L2-026 #2).

## L2-022: Guided lesson
**Traces to:** L1-020

A lesson is an ordered list of 3-6 activities defined in content, shown as a path. The child never chooses what comes next: finishing an activity returns to the path with the next one current, "Up next" and its name spoken and shown, and one touch plays it (ADR 0025).

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a lesson of 5 activities, when the child finishes activity 1 and touches Next after its brief praise, then the path shows activity 1 done and step 2 current by shape and colour, and "Up next" and activity 2's name are spoken and shown; one touch on Play starts activity 2.
2. Given a lesson in progress, when the child leaves and returns on the same device, then it resumes at that activity; a stored position that is missing, of an old version, damaged or no longer matching the lesson is treated as not there and the lesson starts at step 1.
3. Given a lesson in progress, when the child touches Home on the path, then the lesson pauses and Home shows with no confirmation dialog; leaving an activity of the lesson returns to the path at the same step.
4. Given lesson content that names an unknown activity, when the lesson runs, then that step is left off the path and a client warning naming only the lesson and the activity (no child data) is logged.
5. Given the child finishes the last activity, when the path shows, then every step is done, "Lesson done! You played every game." is spoken and shown, and one touch returns Home.

## L2-023: Check game
**Traces to:** L1-021

A check game presents 5-8 mixed rounds from what the child has practised and ends with pictures of what they can do.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the check game ends, when results are shown, then only skills answered correctly are shown, as earned pictures; no score, percentage, red mark or count of misses is shown.
2. Given a wrong answer in the check game, when it happens, then the normal retry rule (L2-026) applies.
3. Given results, when saved, then they stay in on-device storage only and are erased by "clear progress" in the grown-up area.
4. Given no practice history on the device, when the check game starts, then its rounds come from the first lesson's activities.

---

## L2-024: Home screen
**Traces to:** L1-022

Home shows two groups, Count and Read, each a picture spoken when touched. Choosing a group shows its activities as pictures, at most 6 per screen; a group with more shows 5 a screen and a More picture that is spoken and shows the next screen. Only activities that exist are shown. The first touch on Home unlocks sound.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given Home, when the child touches the Count picture, then "Count" is spoken and the counting activities are shown.
2. Given any activity picture, when touched once, then its name is spoken and it opens.
3. Given Home on any viewport, when shown, then nothing scrolls and every target is at least 60 x 60 px with at least 16 px between targets.
4. Given Home, when shown, then guided lessons and the check game each have their own picture.
5. Given Home, when the child touches the Read picture, then "Read" is spoken and the reading activities are shown.
6. Given Home has just opened, when nothing has been touched, then nothing is spoken and no group is chosen.
7. Given a group with more than 6 activities, when it is chosen, then 5 are shown with More; touching More speaks "More" and shows the next screen, and the last screen's More shows the first again.

---

## L2-025: Spoken and shown instructions
**Traces to:** L1-023

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given any round starts, when it is shown, then its instruction is spoken and also shown by a picture or animation.
2. Given any round, when the child touches replay, then the instruction is spoken again.
3. Given the device is muted, when any round is played, then it can be completed from what is shown alone.
4. Given any activity screen, when shown, then no countdown, timer, lives, streak or score is visible.

---

## L2-026: Retry and praise
**Traces to:** L1-023

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given any wrong answer, when it is made, then nothing earned is removed, no harsh sound plays, and a hint is given before the child tries again.
2. Given three wrong answers in one round, when the third occurs, then Pibbo models the correct answer and the round completes.
3. Given a correct answer, when praise plays, then it lasts no more than 1.5 s and a touch on the next control is accepted during it.

---

## L2-027: Content API
**Traces to:** L1-024

An ASP.NET Core (.NET 10 LTS) API serves read-only, versioned content: `GET /api/v1/content/manifest`, `GET /api/v1/activities/{id}`, `GET /api/v1/lessons/{id}`, plus static recordings and pictures.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a valid activity id, when `GET /api/v1/activities/{id}` is called, then 200 is returned with JSON matching the published OpenAPI schema.
2. Given an unknown id, when called, then 404 with an RFC 9457 problem-details body is returned.
3. Given an id not matching `^[a-z0-9-]{1,64}$`, when called, then 400 problem details is returned and no lookup is made.
4. Given the manifest, when returned, then it carries a content version and an ETag; a request with a matching If-None-Match gets 304.
5. Given the OpenAPI document, when the build runs, then the Angular client types are generated from it and the build fails if they drift.

---

## L2-028: Backend holds nothing about a child
**Traces to:** L1-024

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given every public endpoint, when called with POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE, then 405 is returned; only the authenticated admin endpoints (L2-031) accept writes.
2. Given any request, when logged, then the client IP is omitted or truncated, and no query string, cookie or device identifier is stored.
3. Given any public response, when inspected, then it has no Set-Cookie header.
4. Given the backend is added, when reviewed, then an ADR in `docs/adr/` records that it serves content only and holds nothing about a child.

---

## L2-029: Nothing about a child leaves the device
**Traces to:** L1-025

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a full Playwright run of every activity, when network traffic is captured, then only GET requests to the Pibbo origin are made and none carries progress or answers.
2. Given the app, when audited, then it requests no microphone, camera, location or notification permission.
3. Given the built app, when scanned, then it has no third-party analytics, advert, social or tracking script, and no link to another origin.
4. Given the grown-up area, when "clear progress" is confirmed, then all Pibbo progress in localStorage and IndexedDB is erased.

---

## L2-030: Transport and header hardening
**Traces to:** L1-026

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given an HTTP request, when received, then it is redirected to HTTPS, and responses carry HSTS with max-age of at least 31536000.
2. Given any response, when inspected, then it carries a Content-Security-Policy with `default-src 'self'`, no `unsafe-inline` script, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, plus `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`.
3. Given a cross-origin request, when made, then CORS allows only the configured frontend origin.
4. Given more than 300 requests in one minute from one client, when the limit is passed, then 429 with Retry-After is returned.

---

## L2-031: Content administration security
**Traces to:** L1-026

Staff edit content through authenticated endpoints under `/api/v1/admin`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given no token, when an admin endpoint is called, then 401 is returned.
2. Given a valid OIDC token without the `content-editor` role, when called, then 403 is returned.
3. Given an uploaded file, when received, then its type is checked by content sniffing (audio/mpeg, audio/ogg, image/webp, sanitised image/svg+xml), its size is capped (audio 1 MB, image 2 MB), and anything else is rejected with 415 or 413.
4. Given text content (words, sentences), when saved, then it is validated against a character allow-list and a maximum of 80 characters, and Angular renders it without bypassing sanitisation.
5. Given the CI pipeline, when it runs, then `dotnet list package --vulnerable` and `npm audit` fail the build on high or critical findings.

---

## L2-032: Load and response performance
**Traces to:** L1-027

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a mid-range phone on a throttled 4G profile, when Home loads cold, then Largest Contentful Paint is at most 2.5 s and the initial JS is at most 250 KB gzipped.
2. Given any touch on a target, when handled, then visual feedback appears within 100 ms, and INP is at most 200 ms at p75.
3. Given an activity is opened, when its first round shows, then its recordings are preloaded so the first touch plays within 150 ms.
4. Given the content API at 500 requests per second, when load-tested, then p95 latency is at most 200 ms and the error rate is below 0.1%.

---

## L2-033: Offline after first use
**Traces to:** L1-027

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given an activity opened once while online, when the network is lost, then it still plays fully, recordings included.
2. Given the app is offline, when the child touches an uncached activity, then its picture shows greyed with a no-network symbol and it does not open.
3. Given a new content version, when the app is next online, then the service worker updates content in the background without interrupting a round.

---

## L2-034: Responsive breakpoints
**Traces to:** L1-028

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given XS width (< 576 px) upright, when any activity is shown, then it uses one column with every target visible without scrolling.
2. Given a landscape viewport under 480 px tall, when shown, then the layout is side by side (picture one side, choices the other) with no scrolling.
3. Given SM (>= 576 px), MD (>= 768 px) and LG (>= 992 px), when shown, then the same items are shown larger with more space, not more items.
4. Given XL (>= 1200 px), when shown, then content is centred within a maximum width and no target exceeds 160 px.
5. Given viewports from 320 x 480 px to 2560 x 1440 px, when each activity's first round is screenshotted, then nothing overflows or overlaps and no scrollbar appears.
6. Given a round in progress, when the device rotates, then the round state (counted things, filled slots) is kept.

## L2-035: Touch robustness
**Traces to:** L1-028

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given any activity, when the child pinches, double-taps or long-presses, then the page does not zoom, select text or open a context menu, and no answer is made.
2. Given a palm resting on the screen edge, when another finger taps a target, then the tap is handled and the resting contact activates nothing.
3. Given an activity picture has been touched and is opening, when another target is touched, then the touch is ignored and only the first activity opens.

## L2-036: WCAG 2.2 AA and keyboard
**Traces to:** L1-029

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given every screen in every state, when scanned with axe-core, then there are no serious or critical violations.
2. Given a desktop with keyboard only, when navigating, then every target is reachable in a logical order with Tab or arrow keys, focus is visible at 3:1 contrast or more, and Enter or Space activates it.
3. Given text and essential graphics, when measured, then text contrast is at least 4.5:1 and graphics at least 3:1.
4. Given a screen reader, when a target is focused, then it announces its spoken name (for example "letter m, sound mmm").
5. Given prefers-reduced-motion, when set, then every non-essential animation is removed and every activity can still be completed.
6. Given any state (counted, correct, current), when shown, then a shape or icon shows it as well as colour.
